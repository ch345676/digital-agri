# Real imagery used in the demonstration

These are genuine photographic / remote-sensing images, not AI-generated pictures. They are reference imagery, not captures from the demonstration project's devices or alert events. Crop labels, areas, device markers and routes are simulated.

## Farm base image

- File: `farm-satellite.jpg`
- Source: [Esri World Imagery](https://services.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer)
- Attribution returned by the service: Esri, Vantor, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community.
- Requested area: farmland west of Davis, California, USA. Geographic request bounds: -121.825, 38.529, -121.801, 38.540 (EPSG:4326); export EPSG:3857; 1800 × 1008 pixels.
- [Original export request](https://services.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer/export?bbox=-121.825,38.529,-121.801,38.540&bboxSR=4326&imageSR=3857&size=1800,1008&format=jpg&f=image)
- Saved on 2026-09-21. Acquisition date is not asserted. The base may contain imagery acquired at different times. Display labels and route graphics are application overlays.
